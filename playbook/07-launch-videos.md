# The first 10 launch videos: shot lists

Every one of these is a format that already works on dog-tok. The job is to record them, not to
improve them. Record all ten over one afternoon and one evening walk (the 5:30pm notification is
real, so be by the door at 5:15), then post 1–2 a day across two accounts.

Rules for all ten:
- Dog in frame within 2 seconds. The dog is the hook. No intro, no logo.
- Product visible right after: by second 3 at the latest.
- Text hook on the first frame, 6 words or fewer where possible.
- 7–15 seconds. Longer only if the format needs it.
- CTA is the app name on screen at the end, nothing else. Don't say "link in bio".
- Captions on. Most people watch on mute.
- Never show an off-leash dog near roads. Leash on, or a fenced / clearly safe space.
- No claims about health outcomes. Not "lives longer", not "loses weight", not "calmer".
  We say what the dog's guideline is and whether the walk happened. That's all.

### 1. The demo (the one everything else points back to)
**Hook text:** "One tap. That's the whole habit."
**Shots:**
1. (0–2s) Dog at the door, lead in mouth or staring. Phone buzzes.
2. (2–4s) Lock screen: "Has Rex had a walk today?" Thumb long-presses, taps "Walked ✓".
3. (4–7s) Open the app. Ring fills around Rex's photo. Streak ticks 29 → 30. Confetti.
4. (7–8s) End card: app name.
**Why it works:** Nothing to explain. This is the App Store preview too (cut shot 1 for that).

### 2. The reveal
**Hook text:** "I asked how much walking he needs."
**Shots:**
1. Dog looking at camera. Cut to screen recording: medium, mixed, adult, "20 min a day".
2. Reveal screen: "Rex needs about 60 min a day." Cut to your face. Hold 1s.
3. "243 hours a year Rex is missing." Cut to the dog's face. Hold 1s.
4. End card.
**Note:** The footnote "general guideline" stays visible in the recording. Don't crop it.

### 3. Day 1 of Walk Your Dog Week
**Hook text:** "Day 1 of Walk Your Dog Week."
**Shots:**
1. Clip the lead on. One sentence: "Seven days, seven proper walks. This is how I'm counting."
2. Tap "Start a walk". Timer runs. Jump cut to the end of the walk: "Finish". Confetti.
3. "Day 1 with Rex" card. "I'll post the 3 and the 7."
4. End card.
**Note:** Post Oct 1. Then post the 3-day and 7-day cards on schedule. Three videos from one setup.

### 4. POV: 5:30
**Hook text:** "POV: he's been staring since 4pm"
**Shots:**
1. Laptop in foreground, dog out of focus behind it, staring. Rack focus to the dog.
2. Phone buzzes: "Has Rex had a walk today?" Look at phone. Look at dog. Close laptop.
3. Tap "Start a walk". Door opens, dog bolts to the end of the lead.
4. End card: "Good Walk."

### 5. Slideshow: 5 signs
**Hook slide:** "5 signs your dog wants a longer walk" (photo of the dog on every slide)
**Slides:**
1. The 9pm zoomies are a scheduled event.
2. They know the word. You now spell it.
3. They sit by the lead and sigh. Loudly.
4. "Round the block" takes four minutes because you've both memorised it.
5. Slide 5: the home screen, Rex in the ring, 30-day streak. "Give the walk a number."
**Music:** whatever's trending that week. Slideshows live on the sound.
**Note:** Behaviour jokes only. Nothing that sounds like a symptom.

### 6. Collar vs. phone
**Hook text:** "A GPS collar is $100+ a year."
**Shots:**
1. Dog on a walk, normal collar. Text: "I don't need to know where he is. He's right here."
2. Cut: Good Walk paywall. "$19.99. No collar. One tap a day."
3. Home screen demo, 3s.
4. End card.
**Note:** Keep it factual. Collars solve a real problem (lost dogs); we solve a different one.
Cheapest yearly plan checked 18 Sep 2026: Tractive Basic, $108 (`playbook/12-sources.md`). Check again
before posting, and don't show a competitor's logo.

### 7. The missed day
**Hook text:** "What a missed day looks like here"
**Shots:**
1. Rain on the window. Dog asleep on the sofa. Text: "Yesterday: didn't happen."
2. Home screen: streak 0, "47 miles · 24 hours · 41 walks" still there.
3. Caption: "No sad dog. No guilt screen. Just today." Tap "Start a walk".
4. End card.
**Why:** People quit streak apps the day the streak breaks. Show there's nothing to dread.

### 8. Tuesday vs. Tuesday
**Hook text:** "Same dog. 15 minutes vs. 60."
**Shots:**
1. Evening, dog pacing the living room with a toy. Text: "15 min day".
2. Cut: same sofa, same time, dog flat out asleep. Text: "60 min day". Ring full on the phone.
3. End card.
**Note:** It's a joke about a tired dog, not a behaviour claim. Don't caption it as one.

### 9. The widget
**Hook text:** "My Home Screen is my dog's face"
**Shots:**
1. Dog's actual face. Match cut to the Home Screen widget: Rex's photo, ring, [Start a walk].
2. Tap the button. App opens on the timer. Lead clips on.
3. End card.

### 10. Founder, day 1
**Hook text:** "I built an app so his walks can't get skipped"
**Shots:**
1. Talking head with the dog in your lap. "Fitness apps track me. Collars track where he is.
   Nothing tracked the one thing I promised him. So: one tap, his face, a streak."
   (demo plays picture-in-picture)
2. "I'll post every day of Walk Your Dog Week. Day 1: 0 users."
3. End card.
**Why:** Build-in-public series give you a reason to post daily and a story people follow.

## What to measure after the first 20 posts

Per video: views, profile taps, App Store page views (App Store Connect → Analytics → Sources),
installs, trial starts. Put them in one spreadsheet. The video with the best **installs per 1,000
views** wins, regardless of total views. Make ten more of that one. Dog videos inflate views;
this ratio is the only honest number.
