# Creator outreach

## Who to DM

- 5k–50k followers on TikTok or Instagram Reels.
- At least two videos over 500k views in the last 90 days. Proof they can stop the scroll.
- The dog is the star and the owner films walks already. Talking to camera or clean
  screen-recording demos both work; "aesthetic vlog" doesn't.
- Niches: dog-tok, "day in the life of my dog", rescue / adoption accounts, new-puppy owners,
  fitness walkers ("hot girl walk" with dog), dog trainers, vet techs (careful: no medical
  claims, and nothing that sounds like a professional endorsement of the numbers).

Aim for 30 DMs to land 10 creators. Small creators answer DMs; managers don't.

## First DM (keep it under 60 words)

```
Hey [name], [dog's name] is exactly who my app is for. It's called Good Walk: it gives your
dog a daily walk target and turns it into a streak, with their photo on everything.

Would you do 3 short videos over 2 weeks? Paid, plus a bonus per trial your code drives.
Can send the app + a 1-page brief today.
```

## The brief (send as one message after they say yes)

```
THE APP
Good Walk asks "Has [dog] had a walk today?" at a time you pick. Tap Walked, or start the walk
timer. It fills a ring around your dog's photo, counts the streak, the miles and the hours,
and gives you a card with your dog on it at 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days. A missed day is just a
missed day. No collar, no map, no account.

YOUR CODE: [CODE] — free month for your audience, we track trials from it.

WHAT TO MAKE (3 videos, 7–15s each)
1. Your version of the demo: dog at the door → notification → tap Walked → ring fills →
   streak ticks.
2. Your version of one of these: "I asked how much walking [dog] needs" (the reveal),
   "Day N of Walk Your Dog Week" with the card, or the "5 signs your dog wants a longer walk"
   slideshow.
3. Your choice, your style.

RULES
- Dog in frame within 2 seconds. App on screen right after. Text hook on frame one.
- Don't explain features. Show the tap and the ring.
- Dog on a leash or in a clearly safe space. Never off-leash near a road.
- No health claims. Don't say it makes dogs healthier, calmer, thinner or live longer. The
  walk target is a general guideline, not vet advice; if you mention it, say "about".
- Say the name once. No "link in bio".
- Post from your account; we may boost the post as a Spark Ad (you keep the views).

REFERENCES
[3 links to videos that already worked in this category]
[Screen recordings of the app: link to shared folder]

PAY
$[150–300] per video on posting, plus $2 per trial start from your code, paid monthly.
```

## Rescues and shelters (different ask)

No pay, no brief. Offer free lifetime codes for staff and volunteers and a "7 walks in 7 days"
post idea for Walk Your Dog Week featuring adoptable dogs. If they post, great. If they just
use it, also great; volunteers are dog owners with friends who are dog owners.

## Tracking

- One App Store offer code per creator (App Store Connect → Subscriptions → Offer Codes).
- Spreadsheet: creator, video link, post date, views at 48h, trials from code at 7 days, cost.
- Trials per $100 spent is the number. Re-book anyone above the median. Drop anyone below
  after three videos.

## Follow-ups

Day 3 no reply: "Bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to just send the app so you and [dog] can try it."
Day 7 no reply: move on.
