# Naming: why "Good Walk"

Decided 18 September 2026. The app is **Good Walk**. The line is **Every dog deserves a good
walk.** The repo folder slug stays `dogwalk/`.

## What the name has to do

1. Say the promise in plain words: the dog gets a proper walk, every day.
2. Survive a 3-second TikTok caption and a 7-second demo.
3. Be clear of the App Store results for "dog walk", "dog walk tracker" and "dog walking".
4. Not sit on someone else's trademark.

## Why Good Walk

- It echoes "good boy" / "good girl". The warmest two words in the category, one letter away.
- It says the promise. Not "a walk" (round the block counts), a *good* walk (the dog's target).
- Two plain words people already say out loud: "did Rex get a good walk today?" The
  notification, the widget and the share card all sound like the owner talking.
- It's about the dog, not the human. No "fit", no "steps", no "tracker" in the name.
- "Good Walk" was not found in App Store search results for dog walk trackers in a web search
  on 18 Sep 2026. That's a web search, not the store itself: **re-check on a phone before
  submission**, and check for golf apps using the phrase ("a good walk spoiled").
- Button copy and share cards write themselves: **Start a walk** · *30-day streak with Rex* ·
  *47 miles walked with Rex*.

App Store title: `Good Walk: Dog Walk Streak`. Subtitle: `Daily walk goal for your dog`.

## Rejected

| Name | Why not |
|---|---|
| Walkies | Taken. "Walkies: Pet Sitting & Walking App" is a business platform for professional dog walkers (found 18 Sep 2026). Wrong audience, same search results. |
| Walky | "Walky – Dog Walk Tracker" exists, is free and has streaks. Too close in name and function. |
| TreatWalk | Exists. |
| Heel | Reads as a command or a shoe. Obedience-training connotation; we're not a training app. |
| Leashed / Leash | Negative ("kept on a leash"), and several leash-named apps already. |
| Pawdometer | A pun. Hard to spell, hard to say in a video, reads as a gimmick. |
| Fetch | Crowded: Fetch Rewards, Fetch pet insurance. Unwinnable search term. |
| Strol / Trot | Generic, not dog. Could be a running app or a pram brand. |

## Domain

`goodwalk.app` is the choice. **Not bought yet** (Nick buys it at Cloudflare Registrar). A
registry "not found" is a strong signal, not a guarantee: a name can still be premium-priced or
reserved. Check the price at checkout. If it's gone or premium, fall back in this order:

| Candidate | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **goodwalk.app** | **Chosen.** Exact match, shortest, matches the bundle ID. |
| getgoodwalk.app | Standard "get<name>" pattern; matches the fallback handle. |
| goodwalkapp.com | Fine as a redirect, weak as the primary. |
| agoodwalk.app | Reads nicely, but one more thing to spell out loud. |

If the fallback domain is used, the bundle IDs don't need to change; they're already registered
by then and nobody sees them.
