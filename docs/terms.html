What Good Walk does

Good Walk lets you set a daily walk target for your dog, log walks, time walks with an estimated distance, and see streaks, totals and milestones based on what you log. It is a tool to support your own choices. Distances and targets are estimates.

Not veterinary advice

Good Walk is not a veterinary service and does not provide veterinary or medical advice, diagnosis or treatment for animals or people. The daily walk target is a general guideline based on the size, breed type and age you enter. It does not know your dog. Ask your vet how much and what kind of exercise is right for your dog, especially for puppies, seniors, flat-faced breeds and dogs with health conditions, and in hot or cold weather. If your dog seems unwell, tired or in pain, stop and contact a vet.

Follow local leash laws, keep your dog under control, watch the road and the weather, not your phone, and don't let a streak talk you into a walk that isn't safe.
