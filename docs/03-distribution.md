# Distribution — start before the app is approved

## The rule

Don't invent formats. Dog content is the most proven short-form on the internet. Copy the
*format*, change the story, post at volume, and judge by the tier ladder, not by views. Dog
videos get views for free; views are not the goal.

## Tier ladder (how we judge every video)

| Tier | Signal | What to fix if stuck here |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Low views | Hook / first frame. Is the dog in frame in the first 2 seconds? |
| 1 | Views | Product appears too late or is unclear. Show the tap and the ring right after the dog. |
| 2 | Views + engagement | It's a cute dog video, not an app video. Make the demo the story. This is the tier dog content gets stuck on. |
| 3 | + Downloads | Onboarding / paywall. Marketing did its job. |
| 4 | + Conversions | Scale this concept. |
| 5 | + Low churn | The business works. Do more of exactly this. |

## Formats that already work in this category (make our versions)

1. **Screen-recording demo** — dog at the door, 5:30pm notification, tap "Walked ✓", ring fills
   around the dog's photo, streak ticks. 5–8s.
2. **"Day N" series** — "Day 12 of walking Rex every single day" with the app's streak as the
   proof. Daily.
3. **The reveal** — "I asked an app how much walking my dog actually needs" → the 60 vs 22
   screen. Reaction face, then the dog's face.
4. **Slideshow / text-on-image** — "5 signs your dog needs a longer walk" → slide 5 is the app.
   (Behaviour signs only: zoomies at 9pm, staring at the lead. No health claims.)
5. **POV / skit** — "POV: it's 5:30 and your dog has been staring at you since 4."
6. **Day in the life of my dog** — the walk is the middle of the video; the ring filling is the
   payoff.
7. **Founder build-in-public** — "I built an app so my dog's walks can't get skipped. Day 3."

## 20 hooks to test (first frame text, over a shot of the dog)

1. Most dogs get about 20 minutes a day. Here's what mine needs.
2. I asked an app how much walking my dog needs. It said 60.
3. My dog can't ask for a longer walk. So my phone does.
4. Day 1 of Walk Your Dog Week. Here's my tracker.
5. POV: it's 5:30 and your phone asks "Has Rex had a walk today?"
6. 243 hours a year. That's what my dog was missing.
7. 47 miles with Rex. My phone made me a card.
8. A GPS collar is $100 a year. I just wanted to log walks.
9. I put my honest number in. It said I'm at a third.
10. The streak isn't mine. It's his.
11. 30 days in a row. Here's what my Home Screen looks like.
12. Stop promising your dog "a long one at the weekend".
13. Every dog deserves a good walk.
14. My dog's walk target vs. what he was actually getting.
15. One tap. That's the whole habit.
16. My widget is my dog's face and one button.
17. The card I get every milestone. Yes I post it.
18. Tractive shut down its walk app. This is where I went.
19. Strava has no idea my dog exists.
20. What a missed day looks like in the least judgmental app on earth.

## Creator brief (paste to small creators)

- Who: 5k–50k followers, at least two videos over 500k views, the dog is the star of the
  account. Niches: dog-tok, "day in the life of my dog", rescue / adoption accounts, new-puppy
  owners, fitness walkers ("hot girl walk" with dog), dog trainers, vet techs (careful: they
  must not make medical claims on our behalf).
- Ask: 3 videos over 2 weeks, formats 1, 2 or 3 above. We provide: promo code, hooks list,
  3 reference videos, screen recordings, their dog's milestone card.
- Pay: flat $150–300 per video plus a $2 bounty per trial start via their code (tracked with
  App Store offer codes / promo links).
- Don't ask them to be original. Ask them to make *their* version of what already works.

## Walk Your Dog Week (Oct 1–7) and Walk Your Dog Month (January)

- Countdown content from Sep 24: "7 days to Walk Your Dog Week, here's how I'm tracking it."
- Daily "Day N" post from the founder account Oct 1–7, then keep the streak going all month.
  The ring with the dog's face is the content.
- Pitch 20 dog accounts and 10 rescues: "7 walks in 7 days, track it with this, post the card."
  Rescues post adoptable dogs on walks anyway; offer free lifetime codes for their volunteers.
- Reddit: r/dogs, r/puppy101, r/DogTraining (read each sub's rules; most ban self-promotion;
  value-first posts, not links). r/reactivedogs: do not promote, ever. Walks are a hard subject
  there and a streak app is the wrong message.
- December: the January content bank. Every post that worked in October, re-cut with "new
  year, same dog, longer walks". Ask October users for reviews before Dec 15 so the listing has
  stars by Jan 1.
- Spring: "first warm weekend" content. Same formats, new light.

## Paid (only after organic reads)

- Take the 3–5 concepts that produced *trials*, not views.
- $20/day per concept on TikTok Spark Ads first (boosting the organic post keeps social proof).
- Creative policy: no health-outcome claims ("longer life", "weight loss", "calmer dog"), no
  off-leash dogs near roads, no competitor logos in paid creative.
- Kill anything above $2.10 CPI after $100 spend. Scale anything under $1.35.
